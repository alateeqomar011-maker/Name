# Murattil — مُرتِّل

Practise Quran recitation with feedback measured on your own voice:

- **Tajweed Coach** — recite an ayah range; the Quran speech recogniser aligns every word and reports
  missed, different and repeated words with the exact ayah and word, step-by-step corrections and
  the reference recitation to compare. Each new attempt is compared with the previous one.
- **Reciter technique** — record the same passage as your favourite reciter (e.g. Ahmed Al-Nufais)
  and get word-by-word measurements of pace, breath, melody, madd lengths and tone, with both
  recordings to listen to.
- **Voice Studio** — hear your own recitation in a reciter-inspired style (your voice, clearly
  labelled; never presented as the reciter's voice).

Source code and measured accuracy: https://github.com/alateeqomar011-maker/Name/tree/claude/quran-voice-tajweed-app-m8ylt4/murattil Automated feedback can miss mistakes and flag
correct recitation — have a qualified teacher confirm your recitation.
